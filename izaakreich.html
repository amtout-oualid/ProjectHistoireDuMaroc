About us

We are proud that after 150 years, we have been able to continue the legacy of the ancient glass masters from Staré Hutě and have started production in a modern glassworks bearing the name of the native, great glassmaker, and founder of a significant glassmaking dynasty, Mr. Izaak Reich.

Glassworks

All Izaak Reich glasses are produced in our modern glassworks, constructed in 2022 on the historic farm Zikmundov, near the original glassworks where Izaak Reich commenced his glassmaking journey.
